I am a master's graduate of the University of Michigan. In the Hybrid Dynamic Robotics Lab, advised by Prof. Xiaonan Huang, I worked on sim-to-real transfer for novel robot morphologies. I am also a technical founding member of the lab's spin-off, with Prof. Huang, where we build tactile dexterity for the agricultural sector.

Before that, I received my bachelor's degree from Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University and the University of Liverpool, graduating with High First Class Honours.

I was also very fortunate to be advised by Prof. Yuhao Chen at the University of Waterloo, where I conducted research on embodied perception and reasoning for manipulation [1] and navigation [5]. In industry, I was a research engineer at X-Humanoid, working on perceptive whole-body parkour and confined-space traversal [2] for full-size humanoid robots, and at InstAdapt, where I built a data engine for humanoid data and predictive locomotion [3].

I pursue Deployable humanoid whole-body control, including athletic intelligence and loco-manipulation.
